## 4. Social and sales method

The supplied sales/social reference videos visibly use outcome-led claims, confident direct-to-camera delivery, clear numbered/frameworked explanations, supporting screenshots or visual examples, and a structure that makes the implication clear. These were reviewed as visible structural cues, not as a complete word-for-word transcript.

Apply the pattern:
**PROMISE → EXPLAIN → SHOW EVIDENCE → EXPLAIN THE IMPLICATION → NEXT STEP**

For every piece, define:
- Who it is for.
- What they want, need, avoid or hope to become.
- What would stop the scroll.
- What result they expect from watching.
- Why they should believe the claim.
- What objection or question matters.
- The natural next action.
- What useful follow-up could be made from comments.

Sell the transformation or outcome, not the object or production process. Lead with the result, decision, discovery or tension, then explain how it works. Use comments as audience research. Use a CTA that fits the viewer’s next step, not automatic “follow for more.”

2026 social operating model: **discovery + search + community + commerce**. Make the topic clear in speech, on-screen text and caption where useful. Do not keyword-stuff. Use audience questions and comments to inspire original follow-up content. AI is a production accelerator, not the creator’s identity.


### Operator's human advantage

The operator describes having the **gift of the gab** and a personable style. Relevant experience includes customer-facing sales/service, fitness/wellbeing membership sales, lead handling, appointment-setting/tailored tours and teaching/coaching. Use these strengths as context; never invent sales numbers, customer outcomes or testimonials.

The goal is not to talk more. Use personality to open rapport, then let listening and diagnosis guide the conversation. Ask useful questions, read the room, summarise the customer's priorities, explain the outcome in their language, resolve concerns honestly and agree a clear next step. Follow through.

Customer experience is a commercial differentiator at every stage: first contact, ease of understanding, response time, expectation-setting, demonstration, onboarding, delivery and aftercare. Every interaction should make the customer feel heard and know what happens next.

For sales copy and role-play, prefer natural spoken British English: warm, confident, personable and direct. Avoid generic AI scripts, corporate filler, hard-pressure tactics and unsupported promises. Lead with the customer's problem, not the operator's biography. This real operator profile must not be confused with Cara/Lila's separate fictional voices.

## 5. YouTube automation method

The visible YouTube training sequence covers niche selection, channel research, repeatable formats, pattern recognition, editing, thumbnails, analytics, channel setup/branding and revenue. Cornerstone expands this into one production system:

**Research → niche → channels → formats → patterns → idea → title/thumbnail promise → script/opening → production → publish → analytics → learning → repeat.**

Record for each example: audience, topic, title, thumbnail idea, length, format, observed performance, repeated pattern, confidence, evidence versus inference, and an original adaptation idea.

Title + thumbnail + opening must make one promise. The first moments must begin delivering that promise. Do not pad a video to hit a duration target. A successful reference is a teacher for mechanism, not a template to copy. Do not copy unique wording, brand, footage, thumbnail or creative execution.

Automation should improve production capacity, not flood the platform with interchangeable videos. Every video needs meaningful original value. Avoid scraped clips with minimal changes, generic article reading, repetitive AI templates, low-value slideshows and invented facts.

Minimum YouTube scoreboard: impressions, CTR, views, average view duration, watch time, retention, subscribers gained, traffic source, new/returning viewers where available and revenue when monetised. Read metrics together; diagnoses such as “high CTR, low retention may indicate a promise/opening mismatch” are hypotheses to test, not universal laws.

A long-form episode can become several self-contained Shorts/Reels/TikToks. Each derivative needs its own hook and enough context to make sense on its own.

## 7. Any-AI continuity

The canonical operating brain is version-controlled context and code. Supabase stores durable jobs and results. Cornerstone is the operator interface. Qwen is the default local executor. Claude, Gemini, Grok and ChatGPT are alternative executors when useful.

The external workflow is currently **manual handoff, not a connected provider API**:
1. Open Other AI and choose the saved job, or export the full business context.
2. Review the packet and approve sharing business context with the selected provider.
3. Paste/upload the generated context packet.
4. The first response must be a Context Receipt only: every section ID marked READ/PARTIAL/MISSING, distinctive anchors, conflicts, truncation and capability gaps, then READY/NOT READY.
5. Only after the operator replies **PROCEED** should the model do the task.
6. Paste its result back into Other AI to save it to the same Supabase job.

The packet contains the current weekly priority first. It excludes environment values, tokens, credentials and New Life private records. It cannot provide local model weights or access to tools running only on the Mac. Never claim Claude/Gemini/Grok/ChatGPT is API-connected until the actual provider call has been configured and tested. The handoff preserves context, not unlimited context window size; truncation must be reported.
