# Cara + Lila — 2026 Social + Sales Doctrine

Status: Active
Applies to: public TikTok, Instagram, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, creator research, content generation, captions, commerce tests and growth work.

## Why this exists

The four supplied reference videos point to a repeatable pattern:

- Lead with a strong promise, claim or transformation rather than an introduction.
- Explain value from the viewer's point of view, not from the creator's production point of view.
- Sell the outcome or transformation, not the object or feature.
- Build conviction through a clear point of view, specific examples and a believable reason to believe.
- Use visual proof, screenshots, images or supporting inserts without making the video feel like a slideshow.
- Use the creator's face, voice and personality as the trust layer.
- Turn a journey or before/after transformation into a narrative, not just a list of tips.
- Give one main idea per piece. The viewer should know what they get from staying.
- A useful commercial lesson should feel like content first and selling second.

## Sales psychology applied to content

### 1. Buyer profile becomes viewer profile

For every piece, define:

- Who is this for?
- What do they currently care about?
- What are they trying to get, avoid, understand or become?
- What would make them stop scrolling?
- What would make them believe the claim?
- What action naturally follows?

The viewer is not a lead to pressure. The point is to understand their desired outcome.

### 2. Sell the transformation

Never make the main promise:

- look at this product
- look at this place
- look how pretty this is
- here is a random tip

Prefer:

- what changes for the viewer
- what they will understand after watching
- what mistake they will avoid
- what decision becomes easier
- what identity or outcome becomes more attainable

Commerce follows the same rule: explain why the product improves the experience or solves the problem. Product details support the transformation; they are rarely the transformation.

### 3. Conviction matters

A creator should not make a claim that sounds like borrowed marketing language.

The strongest content has a statement the creator can defend calmly.

Weak:
"Here are 5 amazing things everyone needs."

Strong:
"If the room only looks good in photos, it isn't actually a good room."

The second is a point of view. It creates curiosity, disagreement or recognition without fake outrage.

### 4. Outcome over process

Do not spend the opening explaining how the content was made.

People usually care about the result, decision, discovery, lesson, feeling or transformation.

Show the result first; unpack the mechanism second.

### 5. Objections become content

Every recurring question, disagreement, hesitation or comment is a possible next post.

Do not treat comments only as engagement numbers.

Treat them as a free list of:
- objections
- misunderstandings
- desires
- follow-up questions
- competing opinions
- language the audience naturally uses

The best response to a strong objection is often another piece of content.

### 6. The close is a next step

Do not end every post with "follow for more."

Choose the next step that matches the content:
- save this
- send this to someone
- comment which side you are on
- watch the next part
- see the comparison
- check the linked product
- read the full story
- follow because this is an ongoing series

A CTA should complete the viewer journey, not interrupt it.

## 2026 social direction

Treat social media as four systems at once:

### Discovery

People increasingly arrive with a question, curiosity or intent and then discover adjacent content. TikTok's 2026 forecast explicitly describes curiosity-driven discovery and recommends treating comments, reactions and community participation as part of the creative surface.

### Search

Content must increasingly be understandable as an answer to a real query.

Every useful piece should have:
- a clear topic
- natural spoken keywords
- visible on-screen wording where appropriate
- a caption that names the subject naturally
- a useful payoff

Do not keyword-stuff.

### Community

Comments are not the end of the post. They are raw material for the next post.

Design some content to create:
- a genuine question
- a two-sided choice
- a useful disagreement
- a request for examples
- a reason to add personal experience

Never manufacture fake comment wars.

### Commerce

Discovery increasingly connects to action. TikTok is explicitly expanding the path from discovery to purchase and adding AI-supported tools across the journey. Therefore content should be built so that trust can naturally become action when an offer genuinely fits.

## Originality rule

AI can increase production speed. It cannot become the identity.

Cara + Lila content must have:
- original character perspective
- original wording
- original story/scene
- original or substantially transformed creative execution
- a clear reason the viewer should watch them rather than the source creator

Do not build the account from copied clips, quote cards, stitched reactions or minor edits.

Use references to extract mechanisms, not to reproduce the execution.

## Content architecture

Use a portfolio rather than a single hook type.

### Attention
Goal: earn discovery.

Build:
ordinary setting + sharp statement + curiosity/disagreement + unresolved tension.

### Useful
Goal: earn saves, shares and trust.

Build:
specific problem + clear answer + example + practical payoff.

### Lifestyle
Goal: make people care about the characters.

Build:
ordinary moment + personality/chemistry + one interesting detail + reason to return.

### Commerce
Goal: move an existing interest towards action.

Build:
problem or desire + lived context + product bridge + evidence/fit + natural CTA.

## The content sales funnel

Every account should collectively move people through:

ATTENTION → RECOGNITION → VALUE → TRUST → DESIRE → ACTION → PROOF → REPEAT

Not every post must contain every stage.

The feed should contain enough of each stage that a viewer can move from stranger to follower to customer without the account suddenly turning into an advert.

## Repeatable short-form structure

Default video structure:

0–2s: stop the scroll with the promise, claim or visual change.
2–6s: establish the context and why it matters.
6–20s: deliver the useful point, story or tension.
20–35s: proof, example, contrast or consequence.
Final beat: payoff + natural next step.

For longer clips, extend the middle rather than adding more introductory material.

## Visual execution inspired by the supplied references

- Face-to-camera is a valid trust anchor.
- Keep the creator visually dominant.
- Use supporting screenshots/photos/B-roll when they add proof or context.
- Make on-screen text readable without sound.
- Use clear visual hierarchy: headline, speaker, supporting proof.
- Change visual information when the idea changes; do not cut merely to look busy.
- Use gestures and expression as part of delivery.
- Keep the environment believable for the character.
- Avoid generic AI-luxury backdrops.
- A polished image still needs a human reason to exist.

## Research priority

Research should prioritise:
1. creator formats that produce repeatable attention
2. search questions and topics
3. comments and audience language
4. original formats and recurring series
5. useful content that earns saves/shares
6. commerce content where the product genuinely solves a problem
7. production patterns that improve clarity and retention

The system should record not only what performed but why it may have worked.

Required research fields:
- source
- creator
- topic
- audience
- observed result
- why it stood out
- mechanism
- evidence vs inference
- possible adaptation
- content lane
- search intent where relevant

## Decision rule

When choosing between two ideas:

Prefer the one with:
- a clearer audience outcome
- a stronger reason to believe
- a more specific point of view
- a stronger curiosity gap
- a cleaner visual story
- a more natural next step
- a plausible repeatable series

Do not choose the idea simply because it is louder.
