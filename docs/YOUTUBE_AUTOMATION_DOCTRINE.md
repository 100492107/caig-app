# YouTube Automation — Cornerstone Operating Doctrine

The supplied training clip presents YouTube automation as a system for building faceless channels through a repeatable production process.

## Lessons visible in the supplied video

The visible training sequence covers:
1. YouTube automation — constructing teams/systems to build faceless channels.
2. Length — treat video length as a deliberate variable.
3. Niche — choose the market first.
4. Find channels — research existing channels in the niche.
5. Find a format — identify repeatable formats that attract views.
6. Find patterns — compare multiple winners and look for recurring topic/title/thumbnail structures.
7. Editing — choose editing software and/or editors.
8. Thumbnails — packaging is a specific production stage.
9. Analytics — measure results and use the numbers to improve.
10. Channel setup/branding — the channel surface matters.
11. The video also discusses the YouTube revenue/advertising side.

Some handwritten figures in the clip are not sufficiently clear to transcribe safely, so Cornerstone does not hard-code them as facts.

## Cornerstone workflow

RESEARCH -> NICHE -> CHANNELS -> FORMATS -> PATTERNS -> IDEA -> PACKAGING -> SCRIPT -> PRODUCTION -> PUBLISH -> ANALYTICS -> LEARNING -> REPEAT

Automation is a production system. It is not a reason to produce copy-paste videos.

## Packaging

Treat title and thumbnail as one promise.

Ask:
- Why should this person click?
- What do they believe they will get?
- Is the promise specific?
- Does the opening deliver on it?
- Would the package still work with the channel name hidden?

Do not use clickbait that the video cannot satisfy.

## Retention

Default long-form structure:
- Immediate promise / opening result
- Context without a long introduction
- Escalation / new information / narrative progress
- Proof, examples, contrast or evidence
- Payoff
- Logical next step / next related video

Do not pad a video just to hit a length target.

## Research

For each channel or video studied:
- audience
- topic
- title
- thumbnail idea
- video length
- format
- observed performance
- repeated patterns
- confidence
- adaptation idea

Study mechanisms. Do not copy distinctive wording, branding, footage or execution.

## Originality and monetisation safety

YouTube's current monetisation policy says monetised content should be original and authentic. It specifically identifies repetitive or mass-produced content as potentially ineligible, including generic AI templates and slideshow/templated videos with little narrative or educational value. Reused content also needs meaningful original contribution.

Cornerstone therefore rejects:
- scraped clips with minimal changes
- generic article-reading videos
- interchangeable template videos
- low-value slideshows
- invented facts or sources
- copied titles/thumbnails/branding
- fake performance claims
- synthetic filler used only to increase duration

Automation should increase capacity while keeping each video's substance meaningfully different and valuable.

## Analytics

Minimum scoreboard:
- impressions
- impressions CTR
- views
- average view duration
- watch time
- audience retention
- subscribers gained
- traffic source
- returning/new viewers where available
- revenue once monetised

Interpret metrics together rather than in isolation.

Useful diagnostic hypotheses:
- Low impressions + strong satisfaction: topic, audience or distribution may be the constraint.
- High impressions + weak CTR: packaging is a leading hypothesis.
- Strong CTR + weak retention: promise/opening mismatch or weak delivery is a leading hypothesis.
- Strong CTR + strong retention + limited impressions: topic size, competition or audience fit may be limiting growth.

These are hypotheses, not guaranteed causal rules.

## Production organisation

A mature channel separates:
research -> ideas -> scripts -> narration -> visual sourcing/generation -> editing -> thumbnails -> upload -> analytics -> learning.

One person can do all stages initially. The architecture allows work to be delegated to people, software or AI later.

## AI role

AI can accelerate research synthesis, idea generation, titles, outlines, scripts, visual prompts, thumbnail concepts, QA, analytics diagnosis and repurposing.

AI must not invent evidence, fabricate sources or force every video into the same template.

## Revenue

Possible revenue layers include YouTube advertising once eligible, sponsorships, affiliates, products/services, licensing and lead generation.

Cornerstone revenue planning uses assumptions. It does not treat RPM, views or monetisation eligibility as guarantees.

Estimated ad revenue = monetised views / 1,000 × assumed RPM.

## Integration with Cornerstone

The YouTube system becomes another media engine:

Research -> long-form -> Shorts/clips -> social discovery -> audience -> trust -> commercial action -> measurement -> learning -> next video.

For Cara + Lila, YouTube should remain a creator identity asset rather than becoming a generic faceless channel.
