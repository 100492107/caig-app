# Cara + Lila — Attack Week 01

This is the first public execution week. The purpose is to get a complete portfolio into the market, not to prove the strategy in theory.

## Weekly mix

- 2 × Attention / controversy
- 3 × Useful / educational / interesting
- 2 × Lifestyle / relationship / day-in-life
- 0–1 × Soft commerce, replacing one lifestyle slot only when a verified product path is ready

## Posting order

### Post 1 — Useful
**Format:** Quick take / useful list  
**Concept:** 3 small changes that make an ordinary space feel noticeably better.  
**Job:** Give a practical save.  
**Rule:** Specific examples, no fake expertise or invented claims.

### Post 2 — Attention
**Format:** Duo reaction / café moment  
**Concept:** “We don't actually like brunch.”  
**Job:** Create a genuine WTF/disagreement moment from an ordinary setting.  
**Gate:** Full Attention Gate required before persistence.

### Post 3 — Lifestyle
**Format:** Day in the life  
**Concept:** A normal getting-ready / leaving-the-house sequence where Cara and Lila naturally differ over what matters.  
**Job:** Chemistry and familiarity.  
**Rule:** No manufactured fight.

### Post 4 — Useful
**Format:** Story + recommendation  
**Concept:** What they actually pack for a short city break, with the reason each item earns space.  
**Job:** Practical value and saves.  
**Rule:** Only make factual product claims that are verified.

### Post 5 — Attention
**Format:** GRWM / quick take  
**Concept:** One social expectation they deliberately refuse to follow when getting dressed.  
**Job:** Pattern interrupt + recognisable disagreement.  
**Gate:** Full Attention Gate required before persistence.

### Post 6 — Useful
**Format:** Comparison / carousel  
**Concept:** “Worth paying for vs not worth paying for” in an everyday category they genuinely use.  
**Job:** Teach a decision rule people can reuse.  
**Rule:** Evidence or clearly labelled personal preference; no invented authority.

### Post 7 — Lifestyle or soft commerce
**Format:** Personal moment / story + recommendation  
**Concept:** A real-feeling ordinary moment that naturally leads to one item they genuinely keep coming back to.  
**Job:** Build personality first; monetise only when the product, destination and tracking path are verified.  
**Rule:** Never lead with the sale.

## Production standard

Every post must pass identity continuity, scene logic, believable behaviour, platform-native framing and anti-slop checks.

Only attention posts use the full Attention Gate.

## Measurement

Record these by lane after publishing:

- Views
- Comments
- Shares
- Saves
- Profile visits
- Follows
- Link clicks where applicable
- Conversions / commission where applicable

Do not collapse all posts into one “viral” metric. Judge each lane against its job.

## 14-day review question

After the first 7 posts, identify:

1. Which attention concepts actually created meaningful reaction?
2. Which useful posts generated saves or repeat interest?
3. Which lifestyle posts produced profile/follow behaviour?
4. Whether any product path generated measurable clicks or conversions?
5. Which formats and character combinations deserve another test?

Do not change the entire strategy from one post. Use the first 14 days to identify repeatable signals.
