# Creator Engine

Cara, Lila and Cara + Lila are Track B owned creator assets. Creator work is platform-native rather than YouTube-only.

Supported operating destinations: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok Shop, Affiliate and Fanvue.

Every creator experiment should specify a creator, platform, objective, format, direction and measurable next test. Monetisation is an experiment, not a promise. Use the character source of truth before generating creator-specific work.
