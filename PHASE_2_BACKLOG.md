# Cornerstone AI Enterprises — Current Build Backlog
*September 2026 operating backlog*

This backlog follows the current two-engine architecture. Work is ordered by commercial leverage and demonstrated bottleneck, not by technical novelty.

## Track A — Revenue Recovery

### A1. Universal account model
- Treat customer vertical as context rather than the Track A domain.
- Support business name, decision-maker, vertical, offer, lead source, location, known signals and notes.
- Keep existing CRM storage compatible while presenting accounts, not dealerships, in the active UI.

### A2. Prospecting and discovery
- Search for lead-driven businesses by geography, vertical or market.
- Add verified accounts to the external Recovery workspace.
- Enrich decision-maker/contact information without inventing details.
- Respect suppression and opt-out state.

### A3. Recovery diagnosis
- Capture the lead-to-deal chain.
- Identify the suspected leakage stage.
- Record evidence, baseline KPI and ownership.
- Rank opportunities by recoverability rather than simply by age.

### A4. Recovery messaging
- Prospect-first email and DM sequences.
- Problem-focused language.
- AI-assisted personalisation using verified facts only.
- No pricing in cold outbound.
- No invented results.

### A5. Recovery testing
- Define a small controlled test.
- Record opportunities entering the test.
- Record recovery actions and outcomes.
- Compare against baseline where possible.
- Decide repeat, change or stop from evidence.

### A6. Commercial learning
- Record replies, objections, buyer roles, leakage patterns, pilot conversions, repeat demand and margin.
- Turn repeat observations into Recovery Playbook intelligence.

## Track B — Content Engine

### B1. Source-content ingestion
- Reliable upload to private source storage.
- Media probing with ffprobe.
- Audio extraction with FFmpeg.
- Transcript handoff through the supported local transcription path.
- Representative frame extraction.
- Qwen Vision analysis.
- Qwen narrative/packaging analysis.
- Unified evidence record with source lineage.

### B2. Opportunity discovery
- Research current public demand by selected niche/channel.
- Rank topics and formats.
- Capture repeated evidence, not only isolated viral examples.
- Store mechanism, emotional trigger, audience promise and production feasibility.

### B3. Source analysis
- Analyse hook, opening, title, thumbnail, story structure, pacing, visual rhythm, curiosity loops, payoff and weaknesses.
- Produce an originality plan rather than a paraphrase.

### B4. Long-form reconstruction
- Select a stronger original angle.
- Generate ranked title and thumbnail concepts.
- Build a high-retention spoken script.
- Create chapters and visual timeline.
- Add research/evidence requirements.
- Store the full production brief.

### B5. Derivative factory
- Detect strong moments in the parent long-form.
- Rank Short candidates.
- Generate standalone hooks and context.
- Store source windows and parent lineage.
- Produce platform-native captions/metadata.
- Support reverse flow from a proven Short to a future long-form opportunity.

### B6. Production routing
- Use local/free methods where they meet quality.
- Escalate to premium providers only when justified.
- Preserve provider adapters so rendering can change independently of intelligence.
- Persist final asset URLs and production state.

### B7. Publishing
- Store publication package.
- Keep titles, descriptions, thumbnails, captions and metadata with the asset.
- Prefer authorised APIs/drafts where available.

### B8. Measurement
- Record reach, CTR where available, retention, completion, shares, saves, comments, followers/subscribers, profile actions and monetisation signals.
- Record production cost, time and rework.
- Distinguish public research from owned-account proof.

### B9. Creative DNA
- Store observed winning topic, hook, format, emotional trigger, visual treatment, packaging pattern, audience response and economics.
- Generate controlled variations from validated mechanisms.

### B10. Monetisation
- Test YouTube advertising where eligible.
- Test affiliate offers.
- Test TikTok Shop where available.
- Use Fanvue for appropriate owned creator assets.
- Test sponsorships, subscriptions, products and licensing when audience evidence supports them.

## Deferred until evidence

The following remain future options rather than current architectural requirements:

- large-scale client workspace expansion;
- generic B2B content retainers;
- self-serve SaaS;
- additional creator fleets;
- automatic platform posting where official APIs are not available;
- expensive provider integrations without a demonstrated quality/cost problem.

## Build rule

Before starting a task, identify the stage it improves:

`Discover | Analyse | Build | Multiply | Publish | Monetise | Measure`

If it improves none of these, it is probably not the next task.

## Current highest-leverage experiment

Take one genuinely strong existing video, ingest it, analyse it, build the stronger original long-form package, generate the derivative Shorts, publish, measure and record the result.

That experiment should determine the next Track B bottleneck.
