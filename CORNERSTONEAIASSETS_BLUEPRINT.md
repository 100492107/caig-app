# CornerstoneAIAssets — Track B Business Blueprint

## Positioning

**CornerstoneAIAssets** is Track B of the wider Cornerstone operation.

- **Track A — Cornerstone AI Group:** the existing commercial/car business.
- **Track B — CornerstoneAIAssets:** the content creation, production and content-repurposing business.

CornerstoneAIAssets is both:
1. a production platform used internally by Cornerstone; and
2. a managed content service sold to businesses.

The platform should become a **content operating system**, not a generic AI video generator.

## Core customer proposition

Do not sell clients “AI videos”. Sell the outcome:

> **Give us one source of truth and we turn it into a complete content system.**

A client can provide:
- website / URL;
- product URL;
- product images;
- existing photos / video;
- brand guidelines;
- offers;
- staff / founder references;
- approved claims;
- prohibited claims;
- previous content;
- a simple brief.

CornerstoneAIAssets turns that into a coordinated set of outputs.

## Content tree

## Production modes

### Static image
1 generated visual. Lowest cost.

### Carousel
4–6 coherent images. No video generation required.

### Cinematic motion
One image animated for 5–8 seconds.

### Multi-image motion
2–4 generated/reference images with motion between scenes.

### UGC
Character + product/reference driven content.

### Short-form
5–60 second social content.

### Long-form
3–30+ minute story, documentary, education, review, podcast or client content.

## Cost philosophy

The default goal is **free/local where practical**, not maximum model spend.

Premium hosted generation should be used only when it materially improves the result.

Every production job should expose:
- mode;
- duration;
- outputs;
- compute tier;
- estimated credits;
- provider strategy.

The operator can choose 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 outputs.

## Model strategy

CornerstoneAIAssets should use a provider-adapter architecture.

```text
Creative Engine
      ↓
  model router
      ├── local/open-source
      ├── MoneyPrinterTurbo fallback
      ├── fal adapter
      ├── xAI adapter
      └── future adapter
```

No single provider should become the product's permanent dependency.

The business should not be designed around bypassing a provider's safety controls. The benefit of multiple adapters is cost, quality, reliability and portability.

## Asset memory

Visual assets are durable business data.

Persistent records should exist for:
- characters;
- brands;
- products;
- locations;
- reference images;
- generated images;
- generated videos;
- logos;
- style guides;
- voice profiles;
- previous outputs.

Cara and Lila should have reusable reference profiles. Products should support descriptions, URLs, images, selling points and claims policy.

## Long-form production

Long-form is a first-class Track B capability rather than an afterthought.

A long-form job should support:
- story structure;
- chapters/scenes;
- narration;
- B-roll / reference assets;
- captions;
- sound design;
- thumbnail planning;
- title / description / metadata;
- automatic derivative planning.

## Clipping engine

A long-form source should be analysed for strong moments and turned into ranked short-form candidates.

Each clip should support:
- selected start/end;
- hook rewrite;
- caption treatment;
- reframing;
- B-roll;
- CTA;
- platform aspect ratio;
- source lineage back to the parent long-form video.

## Commercial model

### Stage 1 — Managed service
Sell monthly content packages to businesses.

Potential offer structure:
- starter content pack;
- growth content pack;
- full content department / retainer.

Pricing should be based on outputs, creative complexity, client service and value — not directly on internal API cost.

### Stage 2 — Hybrid service + platform
Clients receive a workspace and can submit content requests while Cornerstone remains the production operator.

### Stage 3 — SaaS
Open the production system to self-serve customers after the internal workflow is proven.

## Revenue streams

1. B2B content retainers.
2. UGC / product creative packages.
3. Long-form + repurposing packages.
4. Track A content / pack promotion.
5. Affiliate / creator opportunities where legitimate and permitted.
6. Future CornerstoneAIAssets SaaS subscriptions.

## Platform / distribution principle

CornerstoneAIAssets should prioritise export and authorised distribution workflows.

Outputs include:
- MP4;
- image packs;
- carousel packs;
- captions;
- titles;
- descriptions;
- metadata;
- thumbnails.

Official platform APIs or draft workflows can be integrated where appropriate. Avoid browser bots and avoid any workflow intended to evade platform enforcement.

## Moat

The moat is not one video model.

The moat is:
- Creative Engine intelligence;
- persistent character/product/brand memory;
- asset lineage;
- production history;
- repurposing graph;
- client knowledge;
- cost-aware model routing;
- reusable workflows;
- proof generated by Cara, Lila and Cornerstone.

## Success definition

CornerstoneAIAssets should reach the point where one source can reliably become a coordinated content portfolio with a clear cost profile, while maintaining the quality and visual consistency of the existing Creative Engine.
