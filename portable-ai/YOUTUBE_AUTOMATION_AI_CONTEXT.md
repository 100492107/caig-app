# YouTube Automation — Portable Context

## Core loop

RESEARCH → NICHE → CHANNELS → FORMATS → PATTERNS → IDEA → PACKAGING → SCRIPT → PRODUCTION → PUBLISH → ANALYTICS → LEARNING → REPEAT

Automation is a production system, not a licence for copy-paste videos.

## Research

For each channel/video studied record:
- audience
- topic
- title
- thumbnail idea
- length
- format
- observed performance
- repeated patterns
- confidence
- adaptation idea

Study mechanisms. Do not copy distinctive execution.

## Packaging

Title + thumbnail + opening are one promise.

Ask:
Why should someone click?
What do they believe they will get?
Is the promise specific?
Does the opening deliver?
Would the package still work with the channel name hidden?

Never use clickbait the video cannot satisfy.

## Long-form retention

Immediate promise / opening result
→ concise context
→ escalation / new information
→ proof / examples / contrast
→ payoff
→ logical next step

Do not pad a video to hit a duration target.

## Originality

Reject:
- scraped clips with minimal change
- generic article reading
- interchangeable templates
- low-value slideshows
- invented facts or sources
- copied titles, thumbnails or branding
- synthetic filler used only to increase duration

Each video must be meaningfully useful and meaningfully different.

## Analytics

Minimum scoreboard:
impressions
CTR
views
average view duration
watch time
audience retention
subscribers gained
traffic source
returning/new viewers where available
revenue once monetised

Useful diagnostic hypotheses:
Low impressions + strong satisfaction → topic, audience or distribution may be the constraint.
High impressions + weak CTR → packaging is a leading hypothesis.
Strong CTR + weak retention → promise/opening mismatch or weak delivery is a leading hypothesis.
Strong CTR + strong retention + limited impressions → topic size, competition or audience fit may be limiting growth.

These are hypotheses, not laws.

## Production

Research → ideas → scripts → narration → visuals → editing → thumbnails → upload → analytics → learning

One operator can perform all stages initially. The same system can later be delegated to people, software or AI.

## AI role

AI can accelerate research synthesis, ideas, titles, outlines, scripts, visual prompts, thumbnail concepts, QA, analytics diagnosis and repurposing.

AI must not fabricate sources or force every video through one template.

## Revenue

Potential routes include YouTube ads where eligible, sponsorships, affiliates, products/services and licensing.

RPM, views and eligibility are assumptions or observed facts, never guarantees.

## Track B connection

Research → long-form → Shorts/clips → social discovery → audience → trust → commercial action → measurement → learning → next video
